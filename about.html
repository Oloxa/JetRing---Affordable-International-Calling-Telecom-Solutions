Our Story & Mission

JetRing Solutions Ltd is a UK-based telecommunications company focused on delivering affordable and reliable international communication services.

Our mission is to simplify global connectivity by enabling people and businesses to stay connected across borders.

THE ONE THING THAT MAKES JETRING DIFFERENT

Most calling apps need the internet on both ends.
JetRing doesn't.

MOST CALLING APPS

Work through the internet.

The person on the other end needs Wi-Fi, a data plan, and the exact same app installed as you. If any of that is missing, the call never connects.

  • ✕ Needs Wi-Fi or active mobile data
  • ✕ Needs a compatible smartphone
  • ✕ Needs the same app downloaded & registered
  • ✕ Cannot reach standard landlines or offline feature phones
JETRING DIRECT VOICE

Uses voice infrastructure — the same network that carries regular phone calls.

The person you're calling receives it just like any regular phone call. No app. No internet. No smartphone required on their side.

  • Reaches any mobile or landline across 50+ countries
  • Works completely offline on their side — zero internet needed
  • Nothing for them to install, set up, or register
  • Carrier-grade voice clarity without VoIP lag or jitter
